## Generation logic (`_generate_event_day_alternatives`, `_probe_event_day_solution`)

Read closely against the bean's four families (add_table, add_capacity-per-table,
unlock-per-pinned/locked-guest, rule_exception-per-hard-rule): each is probed exactly
once per candidate, through one shared probe helper pinned to one search worker, the
caller's seed and the caller's own `time_limit_s` — no independent budget constant.
`disruption_count` is computed from the caller's original `current` chart vs. the probe's
own returned assignment, which is structurally guaranteed to be 0 for the three
non-unlock families (every other chart guest stays pinned in those probes' models) and at
most 1 for the unlock family (only the one released guest's day-pin/lock is skipped).
`violated`/`violated_weight` are re-derived from the probe's own returned assignment via
`_build_report`, never predicted. The sort key
`(disruption_count, violated_weight, kind, guest_id or "", table_id or "", rule_id or "")`
is total and was observed to tie-break deterministically in the adversarial run below.
`add_table` is correctly gated on `len(event.tables) < MAX_TABLES`. `Event(...)` copies in
every family reconstruct all of `Event`'s actual fields (id, name, tables, guests, groups,
rules, locks) — nothing is dropped or aliased in a way that would let a probe mutate the
caller's object.

ac5 (read-only): verified both by the bean's own test and independently on a harder
adversarial fixture (two hard rules, each separately curable) — `event == deepcopy(event)`
and `current == before` held after the solve in both cases.

## Defect 1: a chart overfill combined with a contradicted lock gets no capacity advice, and the unlock advice cannot work

`_event_day_probed_advice` tries unlocks, then capacity, and never both together. When neither probe settles on feasible it returns `None` and main's un-probed report stands (`hard.py:430-436`). Fixture: t-1 capacity 1, t-2 capacity 3; chart g-0 and g-1 at t-1 (a chart overfill); g-2 charted at t-2 but locked to t-1. Head advises `unlock guest g-0 is locked to table t-1` and `unlock guest g-1 ...`: neither guest has a lock, and there is no capacity advice, so ac3 is violated. Re-solves: unlocking g-2 only is infeasible, capacity t-1=2 only is infeasible, and both together are feasible. Fuzz rate: 47 of 937 and 109 of 1727 infeasible results carried advice that did not restore feasibility, all on events with no hard rules.

## Defect 2: existing reports that already carried a recommendation are rewritten, against the constraint

Over the fuzz, 215 of 937 and 522 of 1727 infeasible results that had a recommendation on main changed on head. Many are bean-demanded fixes of wrong main advice; two classes are not:

- bean-025's capacity text is replaced: t-0 capacity 2, chart g-0 and g-1 at t-0, g-2 locked to t-0. Main: `the event's 1 tables provide 2 seats for 3 eligible guests; add capacity`. Head: `table t-0 holds 3 stored guests against its 2 seats; raise its capacity to 3`. 70 fuzz cases have this shape (capacity advice before, no chart overfill). The constraint says bean-025's capacity advice stays as it is.
- Main's unlock advice already worked, but head changed it (134 cases across the 2000+3000 fuzz runs; one example: main named g-1, g-6 and g-0, head names g-1 and g-6), because `if event_day and not kept_rule_ids:` at `hard.py:433` overwrites the report regardless of what main already produced.

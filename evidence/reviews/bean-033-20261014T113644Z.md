## Defect: a timed-out keep-set probe is treated as proof of infeasibility (requirement_miss)

The bean's own constraint says "a diagnosis probe that runs out of time names nothing
it has not proved." `_probe_satisfiable` (`hard.py:791-798`) returns `False` for both
`INFEASIBLE` and `UNKNOWN` (a timeout). The removal search only acts on a positive
result (`if probe(...)`, meaning "proven satisfiable") so an undecided probe there
correctly names nothing. But the keep-set search's `keep()` helper
(`hard.py:945-951`) is the SAME `probe()`, and `hard.py:955` (`if not keep((item,))`)
and `hard.py:959` (`if not keep(pair)`) treat "not proven satisfiable" as "proven
infeasible" — an undecided (timed-out) probe gets named exactly like a proven one.

Demonstrated: 150 guests, 30 tables of 5, 10 harmless rules, 6 harmless locks, one
real contradictory pair, planning mode:
```
head, time_limit_s=0.2:  rules=('ra','rb')  unlock=[g40,g41,g42,g43,g44,g45]
main, time_limit_s=0.2:  rules=('ra','rb')  unlock=[]
head, time_limit_s=10:   rules=('ra','rb')  unlock=[]
```
At `time_limit_s=0.2`, every keep-set probe touching the 6 harmless locks hit
`UNKNOWN` (traced directly: 6 of 190 probes), and all 6 got named as if proven. At
`time_limit_s=0.08-0.1`, head over-names all 10 harmless rules and all 6 locks (main
also over-names at 0.1 through its own older removal step, but is clean at 0.2 —
head's failure mode persists at a time budget main already tolerates).

**Fix:** a keep-set probe must name an item only when the solve status is exactly
`INFEASIBLE`, never on `UNKNOWN`. The removal direction's `probe()` can stay as-is
(a positive-only check is already safe); the keep-set direction needs its own
strict-equality check.
